Rules

How scoring works

  • You pick the team you think will win each group.
  • If correct, you earn points equal to that team's seed.
  • Example: Seed 3 winner = 3 points.

Tie-breakers

  1. Most Correct Picks
  2. Lowest Fair Play Points

Fair play points

  • Every team accumulates fair play deductions during group play.
  • Your fair play total equals the sum of all teams you selected.
  • Lower fair play total wins the tie-breaker.

Fair play deduction system

Only one deduction can be applied per player per match:

  • Yellow card: −1 point
  • Indirect red (second yellow): −3 points
  • Direct red: −4 points
  • Yellow card + direct red: −5 points
